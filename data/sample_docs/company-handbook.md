# Northwind Robotics — Employee Handbook

## About Northwind Robotics

Northwind Robotics builds autonomous warehouse robots. The company was founded in
2019 in Gothenburg, Sweden, and now employs 240 people across offices in
Gothenburg, Berlin, and Toronto. Our flagship product is the **Atlas-7** picking
robot.

## Working hours and flexibility

Standard working hours are 40 hours per week. Core hours, when all employees are
expected to be reachable, are 10:00–15:00 local time. Outside core hours,
employees may arrange their schedule freely with their team lead.

Remote work is allowed up to three days per week. Fully remote arrangements
require approval from both the team lead and the People team, and are reviewed
every six months.

## Vacation and leave

Employees accrue 30 days of paid vacation per year. Up to 5 unused vacation days
may be carried into the next year; days beyond that are forfeited unless the
manager approves an exception in writing.

Parental leave follows local statutory rules. In Sweden, Northwind tops up
parental pay to 90% of salary for the first four months.

## Expenses and equipment

Every employee receives a home-office budget of 8,000 SEK (or local equivalent)
on joining, renewable every three years. Travel expenses must be submitted within
30 days through the Expenses portal and require a receipt for any amount above
200 SEK.

## Reporting a concern

Concerns about safety, conduct, or compliance can be raised with your manager,
the People team, or anonymously through the Speak-Up portal. All reports are
handled confidentially. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good
faith is grounds for dismissal.
